MouRa app - full package with study materials
=============================================
To publish:
1. Unzip this file. You get a folder containing:
   - index.html  (the app)
   - notes/      (58 PDFs: all 8 semesters of 2017-scheme college
     notes + the official TNAU 2025-scheme syllabus PDF)
2. Go to https://app.netlify.com/drop and DRAG THE WHOLE FOLDER
   (not just index.html) onto the page.
3. Share the link it gives you. Every course card now has a
   green "Download notes PDF" button that saves straight to the phone.

Course Finder has three programme tabs:
- Agri 2017  : your batch's scheme, bundled notes for all semesters
- Agri 2025  : the new NEP/6th Deans' scheme (Sem 1-2 official list,
               closest 2017 notes attached; Sem 3 added when TNAU publishes)
- Horticulture : ICAR scheme with Agri Junction / Agrimoon material links

Do not delete the notes folder - the download buttons point into it.
